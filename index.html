About Our Church

A Place to Seek God and Grow.

Omega Prayer Mission Church Mbuya exists to create an environment where people can encounter God, grow spiritually and discover their purpose.

Through prayer, worship, fellowship, discipleship and the teaching of God's Word, we believe lives can be transformed.

🙏

Prayer

Building a strong relationship with God through prayer and intercession.

📖

The Word

Growing in faith through the teaching and understanding of God's Word.

🎶

Worship

Honouring God through worship, praise and thanksgiving.

❤️

Community

Creating a loving church family where people can belong and grow together.
