WHO WE ARE

A family built around faith and prayer.

Omega Prayer Mission Church Mbuya exists to create an atmosphere where people can encounter God, grow in faith and experience restoration.

Through prayer, worship, Bible teaching, counselling and fellowship, we seek to raise people who live purposefully and impact their communities for Christ.

Our mandate: “RAISING GIANTS FOR CHRIST TO POSSESS NATIONS FOR HIM.”

Omega Prayer Mission serves across Uganda through its branch network, including Mbuya Main Centre, Buwate, Seeta and Gayaza Manyangwa.

Prayer Building a strong relationship with God.
Restoration Helping lives experience renewal.
Community Growing together as one family.
Purpose Equipping people to fulfil their calling.
